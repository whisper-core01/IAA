# Components

Components provide replaceable technical or business specializations outside
the kernels. A component is admitted only when its contract, provenance,
capabilities, limitations, and conformance evidence are explicit.

## Experimental components

- [Collatz Scan](argos/engines/collatz/README.md) — finite numerical and
  statistical experiments. It does **not** prove the Collatz conjecture.
- [Hotel Reservation](argos/business/hotel_reservation/README.md) — hotel,
  cleaning and magnetic-key planning plus customer and restaurant history,
  launched by IRIS and admitted and executed exclusively through ARGOS.
