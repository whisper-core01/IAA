# IRIS

IRIS is the domain-agnostic startup and orchestration kernel. It initiates SOL,
activates ARGOS and AORATOS independently, and computes SOL fractal
representations.

For ARKÉ, IRIS performs the matching between researchers or with people already
present in the user's phone contacts. The associated ingress and egress cross a
SOL, which owns canonical I/O conversion rather than matching.

`runtime/reference_launcher.py` is a bounded executable demonstration: IRIS
activates ARGOS and AORATOS, then submits one already-canonical business
scenario to ARGOS. It reports that executable SOL normalization and AORATOS
data-security capabilities are not implemented.

IRIS must not absorb business logic or a concrete organ implementation.
